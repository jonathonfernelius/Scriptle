Hello! This is Scriptle, a game I (Jonathon A. Fernelius) thought of over the summer of 2026. It is a simple concept: the program pulls a random scripture, and you have to guess which part of the scriptures it's from. You will start by selecting which books of scripture you want to make the pool of possibilities, then you will press start and the game will begin!

If you have any questions, or want to suggest any additions to the game, please feel free to contact me!
